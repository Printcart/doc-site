What this tool does
Background Removal is an AI-assisted tool that separates a product or subject from its background, giving you a clean cutout for marketplace listings, catalog images, and print mockups. Use it when photos were shot on messy or inconsistent backgrounds and you need a consistent transparent or white background across a catalog.
What to expect
It works best on clear subjects with defined edges. Results are AI-assisted, not perfect: fine details like hair, transparent materials, or low-contrast edges may need touch-up, and every cutout should be reviewed before publishing or printing. Questions & answers

    
Before you run a job

  

  

    

      Does the tool remove backgrounds perfectly?
      
No. It is AI-assisted and gives a strong starting cutout, but fine details like hair, glass, or low-contrast edges may need manual touch-up before you publish or print.

    

    

      What image types work best?
      
Photos with a clear subject and defined edges on a plain or contrasting background work best. Busy or low-light scenes are harder and often need review.

    

    

      What do I get by registering?
      
A free Printcart account lets you save cutouts, export full-resolution files, and process images in batches instead of one preview at a time.
